### 📖 理论简介
本著作基于三维凸多面体拓扑结结构、H₃群几何投影框架，系统性构建粒子物理、量子引力、宇宙演化统一理论体系。
完整覆盖精细结构常数起源、电荷量子化、轻子/夸克味混合、CP破坏机制、新物理共振态预测、煤矿液压等工业场景落地应用。
Based on the topological knot structures of three-dimensional convex polyhedra and the geometric projection framework of the H₃ group, this work systematically constructs a unified theoretical framework encompassing particle physics, quantum gravity, and cosmic evolution.
It comprehensively addresses the origin of the fine-structure constant, charge quantization, lepton/quark flavor mixing, mechanisms of CP violation, predictions of new physics resonances, and practical applications in industrial settings such as coal mine hydraulic systems.
